Privacy Policy

Updated November 2026 — applies to the app and the website.

1. Summary

Reptor Player is a video player. We do not build user profiles, we do not show ads, and we do not sell or share data. The app does not collect playback history.

2. Data stored on the device

The app stores only on the device itself: the activation code, the video source configuration entered by the user, and usage preferences. This data lets the app open already configured and can be erased in Settings → Change activation or by uninstalling the app.

3. Website activation

When a device is activated through the website, the activation server keeps the device code and the configuration the user provided (server address and access credentials). This is used only to deliver that configuration to the app. We do not store what the user watches, viewing history, or payment data.

4. What we do not collect

The app does not access the camera, microphone, location, contacts, photos, or device files. We do not use analytics, tracking, advertising, or third-party profiles. There is no social login.

5. Third-party content

Videos are loaded directly from the address configured by the user. That provider may log access according to its own policies. We have no control over, and no access to, those logs.

6. Children

The app is not directed to children and does not collect data from minors. The content shown depends on the source configured by the responsible adult.

7. Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with an updated date.

8. Contact

Privacy questions can be sent through the official site: reptor-player.netlify.app.

Reptor Player does not provide, host, or distribute content. It only plays the source configured by the user.
