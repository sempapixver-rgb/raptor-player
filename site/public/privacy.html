Privacy Policy

Updated November 2026 — applies to the app and the website.

1. Summary

Raptor Player is a video player. We do not build user profiles, we do not show ads, and we do not sell or share data. The app does not collect playback history.

2. Data stored on the device

The app stores only on the device itself: the device identification code and usage preferences. This data identifies the device and can be erased by uninstalling the app.

3. Device registration on the website

When you register the device through the website, we keep the device code and an optional name. This is used only to identify the device. We do not store what the user watches, viewing history, or payment data.

4. What we do not collect

The app does not access the camera, microphone, location, contacts, photos, or device files. We do not use analytics, tracking, advertising, or third-party profiles. There is no social login.

5. Demo videos

The four public sample videos are loaded from the developer's releases page. No personal data is sent to play them.

6. Children

The app is not directed to children and does not collect data from minors. The material shown is the public demo videos included in the app.

7. Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with an updated date.

8. Contact

Privacy questions can be sent through the official site: raptor-player.netlify.app.

Raptor Player does not provide, host, or distribute content. It only plays the demo videos included in the app.
