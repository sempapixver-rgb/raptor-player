Activate your TV

Open Raptor Player on your TV, read the code shown on the screen and enter it here. The device is recognized automatically — nothing to type on the remote.

How it works

  1. Install Raptor Player on your TV and open the app.
  2. On the activation screen, scan the QR code with your phone or write down the MAC code.
  3. Fill in this form with the TV code only.
  4. The device is registered and recognized by its code.

Raptor Player is only a video player. It plays the public sample videos included in the app. By registering the device, you agree to the Terms of Use shown in the app.
